Know Your
Calories.

BMR (Basal Metabolic Rate) is the number of calories your body burns at complete rest. Multiply it by your activity level to get your TDEE — the actual calories you need every day.

🛌
BMR
Calories burned doing nothing
⚡
TDEE
BMR × your activity multiplier
